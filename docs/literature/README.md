# Literature evidence

`crossref/<bibkey>.json` holds the Crossref record of each literature reference added to `paper/references.bib` for the introduction, related work and discussion (retrieved 30 Sep 2026).

- **Where the BibTeX comes from:** the entries are generated from these records, so authors, titles, venues, volumes and pages match the registered metadata.
- **One correction:** the first author of `canalscasals2019` is corrected to the surname "Canals Casals", because Crossref splits the name. The correction is commented in the bib file.
- **What the text may claim:** statements about a paper in the manuscript are limited to what its title or registered abstract supports. Anything more specific must be checked against the full text before submission.
- **Dropped reference:** De Lange et al., TPAMI, was considered but dropped. Crossref holds only its early-access record, without the final volume and pages.
