\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\emph{Second-life batteries.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Reviews:} second-life use has been reviewed from technical, economic and regulatory perspectives~\cite{martinezlaserna2018,hossain2019}, and its challenges catalogued~\cite{borner2022}.
  \item \emph{Lifespan:} the remaining second-life lifespan has been analysed together with its environmental impact~\cite{canalscasals2019}.
  \item \emph{Sorting:} retired cells must be sorted and regrouped before reuse~\cite{lai2021sorting}.
  \item \emph{Public ageing data} from second life remain scarce:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Moy et al.\ released grid-duty data of six cells with a documented first life~\cite{moy2024secondlife,pozzato2022firstlife};
    \item Cui et al.\ released data of eight retired Nissan Leaf cells and proposed online SOH estimation with clustering~\cite{cui2024secondlife};
    \item Wheeler et al.\ aged LFP cells from the fresh state far into second life~\cite{wheeler2025lfp}.
  \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\emph{Data-driven SOH estimation and forecasting.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Early-life prediction:} machine learning predicts cycle life from early-cycle data~\cite{severson2019}.
  \item \emph{Impedance:} EIS combined with Gaussian processes forecasts SOH and remaining life~\cite{zhang2020eis}.
  \item \emph{Diagnostic features:} incremental-capacity analysis and related techniques identify degradation modes~\cite{birkl2017,dubarry2022ica}.
  \item \emph{Knees:} non-linear ``knee'' trajectories complicate forecasting~\cite{attia2022knees}.
  \item \emph{Architectures:} the TFT~\cite{lim2021tft} has been adapted to capacity prediction~\cite{gomez2024tft}.
\end{itemize}

Most such studies train and test on one dataset with random or cell-wise splits and do not examine sequential adaptation.

\emph{Transfer and continual learning for batteries.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Transfer learning} moves SOH models across chemistries and charging protocols~\cite{rochaazevedo2023transfer}.
  \item \emph{Continual learning} has been used for SOH estimation under varying temperature~\cite{choi2025continual}.
  \item \emph{General methods:} continual-learning methods, including regularisation such as EWC~\cite{kirkpatrick2017ewc}, rehearsal~\cite{rolnick2019replay} and distillation~\cite{li2018lwf}, are reviewed in~\cite{parisi2019,vandeven2025continual}. Evaluation follows error-matrix metrics~\cite{lopezpaz2017gem,chaudhry2018rwalk}.
\end{itemize}

We are not aware of a study that evaluates continual SOH \emph{forecasting} for second-life cells across both cell-level and usage-level heterogeneity against strong non-neural references.

\emph{Evaluation practice.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Forecast accuracy:} it should be judged against naive references~\cite{hyndman2006}, and simple statistical forecasters often match or beat machine-learning models~\cite{makridakis2018}.
  \item \emph{Leakage and splits:} leakage is a common source of overoptimistic results~\cite{kapoor2023leakage}, and time-ordered, out-of-sample evaluation is needed for forecasting~\cite{cerqueira2020}.
  \item \emph{Pre-registration:} registering analyses before seeing outcomes separates confirmatory from exploratory findings~\cite{nosek2018}.
\end{itemize}

We follow these practices and report where our study deviates from them.
